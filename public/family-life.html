What This Ministry Does

Family Life supports every stage of the home — from couples preparing for marriage, to parents raising children, to families navigating hard seasons together. We believe a healthy home is one of the church's strongest witnesses, and this ministry exists to help families grow closer to each other and to God.

Through seminars, counseling referrals, and practical resources, Family Life offers a place to ask honest questions about marriage and parenting in a supportive, faith-centered setting.
