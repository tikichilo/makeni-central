What This Ministry Does

Youth Ministries — the Adventist Youth (AY) Society — exists to disciple teens and young adults, giving them a place to grow in faith, build friendships, and discover how to serve God with their own gifts. It's where young people move from just attending church to actually living it out.

Through Sabbath programs, social events, and mission-focused activities, this ministry helps youth own their faith rather than just inherit it. Youth Ministries also oversees the Adventurers and Pathfinders clubs, carrying the same discipleship journey from childhood through the teen years.
