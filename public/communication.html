What This Ministry Does

Communication keeps the church connected — inside and out. This department manages the website, social media, announcements, photography, and livestreaming, making sure members always know what's happening and that the wider community can see it too.

It's also the ministry that helps other departments tell their story well, from flyers for an outreach event to highlight reels from Pathfinder camp.

Activities & Programs

  • videocam Livestreaming and recording Sabbath services

  • campaign Weekly announcements and church bulletin
  • design_services Design support — flyers, posters, and program materials for other departments
