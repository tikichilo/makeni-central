What This Ministry Does

Personal Ministries exists to turn every member into a minister — not just the pastor or the elders. This department trains and organizes members to share their faith through evangelism, Bible studies, and everyday acts of witness in their homes, workplaces, and neighborhoods.

It's the engine behind most of the church's outward-facing work — from door-to-door literature distribution to organizing evangelistic series.

Activities & Programs

  • campaign Organizing evangelistic meetings and revival series
  • home Coordinating home Bible studies and interest follow-up
  • local_library Literature distribution and community outreach drives
  • handshake Welcoming and following up with visitors and new interests
