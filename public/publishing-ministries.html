What This Ministry Does

Publishing Ministries carries on one of the church's oldest forms of outreach — literature evangelism. This department distributes books, magazines, and tracts that share Bible truth, often reaching people who might never walk into a church but will read a book in their own home.

It also supports literature evangelists, whether full-time or volunteer, and helps place faith-building reading material in the hands of the community.

Activities & Programs

  • local_shipping Distributing books, magazines, and tracts in the community
  • school Training and supporting literature evangelists
  • library_books Maintaining a church literature table and lending library
  • mail Coordinating literature ministry with home Bible study follow-up
  • event Book fairs and literature evangelism campaigns
