What This Ministry Does

Men's Ministries brings the men of the church together for honest fellowship, spiritual growth, and hands-on service. It's a space to build real friendships and hold one another accountable — as husbands, fathers, and leaders in the church and community.

From maintenance projects on the church grounds to mentoring younger men, this ministry channels practical skill and strength into service.
