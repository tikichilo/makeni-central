What This Ministry Does

Music Ministries leads the congregation into worship through song — the main choir, praise team, and instrumentalists who serve during Sabbath service and special programs. Music here isn't performance; it's an offering, meant to point hearts toward God.

The department also nurtures musical talent across the church, giving members of all ages and skill levels a place to serve using what they've been given.

Activities & Programs

  • groups Main choir rehearsals and Sabbath service worship leading
  • mic Praise team and vocal ensembles
  • piano Instrumentalist training and accompaniment
  • celebration Special music programs for holidays and church events
  • child_care Music mentorship for children and youth

Want to Get Involved?

Reach out to the department leader directly to join the choir, praise team, or as an instrumentalist.
