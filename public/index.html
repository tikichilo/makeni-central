The Promise of Restoration

This week, we explore the transformative power of God's restorative love. Through the study of the life of David, we see how even in our deepest valleys, the Good Shepherd is working to bring us back to wholeness. Join us as we dive into the depths of grace and the certainty of His guidance.

Our Mission & Values

The mission of the Seventh-day Adventist Church is to make disciples of Jesus Christ who live as His loving witnesses and proclaim to all people the everlasting gospel of the three angels' messages of Revelation 14:6-12, leading them to accept Jesus as personal Saviour and prepare for His soon return.

Guided by the Bible and the Holy Spirit, we pursue this mission at Makeni Central through Christ-like living, communicating, discipling, and service — believing that every person is a valued child of God, deserving of dignity, love, and an invitation into a growing relationship with Him.
