What This Ministry Does

Sabbath School is where our study of Scripture starts each week — small classes for every age, from cradle roll to adults, digging into the current quarterly lesson together. It's less a lecture and more a conversation, where questions are welcome and everyone's voice matters.

Beyond the classroom, Sabbath School also organizes mission offerings and outreach projects that turn what we learn into action during the week.
