Our Beliefs

Founded on the Holy Scriptures, our faith is built upon the Word of God.

Seventh-day Adventists accept the Bible as their only creed and hold 28 fundamental beliefs to be the teaching of the Holy Scriptures. These beliefs are organized into six categories exploring the doctrines of God, man, salvation, the church, Christian life, and last-day events.

I

Doctrines of God

Belief 1

Holy Scriptures

The Holy Scriptures, Old and New Testaments, are the written Word of God, given by divine inspiration and the infallible standard for faith and practice.

Belief 2

The Trinity

There is one God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, a unity of three coeternal Persons, infinite and beyond full human understanding, yet near to every heart.

Belief 3

God the Father

God the eternal Father is the Creator, Source, Sustainer, and Sovereign of all creation, just and holy, merciful and slow to anger.

Belief 4

God the Son

God the eternal Son became incarnate in Jesus Christ. Through Him all things were created; He lived a sinless life, died for our sins, and rose again.

Belief 5

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit is the ever-present power of God, active in creation and Scripture, convicting hearts, and equipping believers with gifts for service.

Belief 6

Creation

God created the heavens, the earth, and all living things in six literal days, and rested on the seventh, establishing the Sabbath as a memorial of creation.

II

Doctrines of Man

Belief 7

The Nature of Humanity

Man and woman were made in the image of God, with dignity and freedom of choice. Through sin, that image was marred, leaving humanity in need of redemption.

Belief 8

The Great Controversy

Behind human history lies a cosmic conflict between Christ and Satan over God's character and law, a struggle in which every heart takes part.

III

Doctrines of Salvation

Belief 9

Life, Death, and Resurrection of Christ

Through His sinless life, sacrificial death, and bodily resurrection, Christ secured forgiveness and eternal life for all who put their trust in Him.

Belief 10

The Experience of Salvation

Salvation is God's gift, received by grace through faith in Christ alone, bringing forgiveness, new birth, and a growing relationship with Him.

Belief 11

Growing in Christ

Through the Spirit, prayer, Scripture, and worship, believers grow daily into Christlike maturity and stand firm against the forces of evil.

"Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path."

Psalm 119:105

IV

Doctrines of the Church

Belief 12

The Church

The church is the community of believers who confess Jesus Christ as Lord, called out from the world for worship, fellowship, and mission together.

Belief 13

The Remnant and Its Mission

In the last days, a faithful remnant keeps God's commandments and the faith of Jesus, proclaiming the everlasting gospel to every nation.

Belief 14

Unity in the Body of Christ

All believers, whatever their background, are one in Christ, joined by the Spirit into a single body of fellowship, service, and shared mission.

Belief 15

Baptism

Baptism by immersion symbolizes union with Christ's death and resurrection, following repentance and faith, and marks entrance into the church family.

Belief 16

The Lord's Supper

Communion in the bread and cup proclaims Christ's sacrifice until He returns, preceded by foot-washing as a symbol of humble service.

Belief 17

Spiritual Gifts and Ministries

The Holy Spirit gives every believer gifts to build up the church and carry out its mission, each member contributing to the body of Christ.

Belief 18

The Gift of Prophecy

Scripture identifies the gift of prophecy as a mark of the remnant church, a gift Adventists recognize in the ministry of Ellen G. White.

V

Doctrines of Christian Life

Belief 19

The Law of God

The Ten Commandments express God's unchanging character and remain the standard for righteous living, revealing sin and pointing us to grace.

Belief 20

The Sabbath

The seventh-day Sabbath, from Friday sunset to Saturday sunset, is God's gift of weekly rest and worship, a memorial of creation and a foretaste of eternity.

Belief 21

Stewardship

Believers are entrusted by God to faithfully manage their time, talents, finances, and the earth's resources, returning tithe and offerings in gratitude.

Belief 22

Christian Behavior

Believers are called to wholesome living that reflects biblical principles of health, modesty, and integrity in every area of daily life.

Belief 23

Marriage and the Family

Marriage is a lifelong covenant between one man and one woman, and the family is meant to reflect love, respect, and God's grace to one another.

VI

Doctrines of Last Things

Belief 24

Christ's Ministry in the Heavenly Sanctuary

Since 1844, Christ has carried out the final phase of His atoning ministry in heaven, a work of judgment that prepares the way for His return.

Belief 25

The Second Coming of Christ

Christ's return will be literal, visible, and glorious, the blessed hope of the church, bringing resurrection and reunion with the righteous.

Belief 26

Death and Resurrection

Death is a state of rest, like sleep, until the resurrection, when the righteous will rise to eternal life at Christ's coming.

Belief 27

The Millennium and the End of Sin

After the Second Coming, the redeemed reign with Christ in heaven for a thousand years, after which sin and evil are finally and permanently ended.

Belief 28

The New Earth

God will recreate the earth as an eternal home for the redeemed, free from sin, suffering, and death, where He dwells with His people forever.
