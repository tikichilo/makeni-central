What This Ministry Does

Stewardship teaches and encourages faithfulness with everything God has entrusted to us — not just money, but time, talent, and health too. This department helps members understand tithing and giving as an act of worship and trust, not obligation.

It also provides practical guidance, from budgeting principles to understanding how tithes and offerings support the church's local and global mission.
