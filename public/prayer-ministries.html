What This Ministry Does

Prayer Ministries exists to keep prayer at the center of everything the church does. From the weekly prayer meeting to a standing chain of intercessors ready to pray for urgent needs, this ministry makes sure no request goes unheard and no burden is carried alone.

It also supports other departments — praying over outreach efforts, special programs, and members going through difficult seasons.
