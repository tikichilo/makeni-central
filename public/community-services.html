What This Ministry Does

Community Services (sometimes known as Dorcas) is the church's hands-on relief arm — providing food, clothing, and practical support to families going through hard times, regardless of their background or belief. It's a direct, tangible way the church lives out its calling to care for the vulnerable.

Much of this work happens quietly, through home visits and a standing store of donated goods ready for whoever needs them next.
