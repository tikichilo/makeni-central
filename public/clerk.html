Church Clerk

What This Ministry Does

The Church Clerk keeps the official record of the congregation — membership rolls, baptisms, transfers, and the minutes of church business meetings. It's a quiet but essential ministry that ensures the church's history and standing are recorded accurately, in line with denominational policy.

The Clerk also prepares statistical reports for the conference and helps members navigate transfers, certificates, and other membership matters.

Activities & Programs

  • groups Maintaining the official membership register
  • description Recording minutes of church board and business meetings
  • swap_horiz Processing membership transfers, baptisms, and certificates
  • query_stats Preparing quarterly and annual statistical reports for the conference
  • archive Preserving church records and historical documents
